🛠 Project Architecture & Workflow

How It Works: From Boot to Clean Air

A simple, four-stage autonomous loop designed for zero-Wi-Fi environments, combining low-cost local sensors, on-chip adaptive calibration, and wireless smartphone overrides.

STEP 01 • CALIBRATE

Boot-Time Room Learning

During the first 6 seconds after power-on, the Arduino samples the MQ-135 sensor 15 times to calculate the room's exact clean-air baseline automatically.

STEP 02 • ADAPT

Dynamic Threshold Shift

Instead of rigid hardcoded numbers, the trigger shifts to Baseline + 120 (capped at 550), protecting allergy-sensitive users in clean rooms without false alarms.

STEP 03 • SENSOR FUSION

Strict AND (&&) Decision Gate

To save power, the 5V relay activates the DC fan only when both conditions occur together: poor air anomaly detected AND human presence confirmed by the PIR sensor.

STEP 04 • BLUETOOTH APP

Wireless Override & Telemetry

A custom Kodular Android app pairs over HC-05 Bluetooth to stream live telemetry wirelessly and send instant manual overrides (ON, OFF, or AUTO).

How Edge Intelligence Powers the System

Rather than relying on cloud servers or static threshold guesses, the microcontroller runs unsupervised statistical baseline learning directly at the edge—adapting automatically to any classroom, lab, or home environment.

WHY EDGE AI?

Zero-Internet Autonomy

All statistical calibration and sensor-fusion inference execute locally on the ATmega328P microcontroller in milliseconds—no Wi-Fi, cloud APIs, or latency required.

ALLERGY & SAFETY BALANCE

Adaptive Sensitivity + 550 Cap

In clean rooms, Baseline + 120 catches subtle smoke or VOC spikes early for sensitive individuals. Meanwhile, the 550 upper cap guarantees activation even in heavily polluted rooms.

HYBRID ARCHITECTURE

Edge ML + Voice NLP

Paired with the Kodular mobile app's SpeechRecognizer, the system combines on-device statistical anomaly detection with cloud natural language voice commands.
